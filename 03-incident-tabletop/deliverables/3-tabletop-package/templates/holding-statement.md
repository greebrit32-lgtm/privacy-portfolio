# Holding Statements — Templates

> _Templates from an independent educational exercise. Fictional organization. Not legal advice. Use only after approval by the Practice Administrator and review by counsel (playbook §8)._

**Rules:** one approved voice; state only confirmed facts; no speculation about scope ("no patient data was affected") until forensics confirms; **no statements about ransom payment**; don't name the attackers or vendors.

## A. Front desk / phone script (staff)
> "We're experiencing a technology issue and are working to restore our systems. Patient care is our priority, and we're [keeping appointments / rescheduling some appointments]. I'm not able to share more details, but I can take your name and number, and someone from our office will follow up."
>
> **Media or reporters:** "I'm not able to comment. Please contact [Practice Administrator] at [main office number]."

## B. Media holding statement (approved spokesperson only)
> "On [date], Agarita Family Medicine identified a cybersecurity incident affecting some of our computer systems. We took immediate steps to secure our systems, engaged outside cybersecurity experts, and notified law enforcement. Our investigation is ongoing. If we determine that patient information was involved, we will notify affected individuals directly as required by law. Patient care continues [details]."

## C. Patient-facing website notice (early, before individual letters)
> "[Date] — We are investigating a cybersecurity incident that affected some of our systems. We are working with outside experts and law enforcement. We will provide updates here and will contact affected patients directly if their information was involved. Questions: [toll-free number]."

*Formal individual breach notices follow HIPAA content rules (§164.404(c)) and are drafted separately (deliverable 5).*
