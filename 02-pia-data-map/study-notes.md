| 2026-11-03 | Privacy impact assessment (PIA): assessing privacy risk **before** a system launches, so privacy is designed in rather than added later ("privacy by design") | Privacy program management — PIAs/DPIAs; privacy by design | _Brittany to write in own words._ **Sourced:** Texas requires a documented "data protection assessment" for targeted advertising, sale of personal data, certain profiling, processing sensitive data, and other heightened-risk processing (Tex. Bus. & Com. Code §541.105(a) [S01]). It must weigh benefits against risks as mitigated by safeguards, and factor in de-identified data, consumer expectations, context, and the relationship (§541.105(b) [S01]). | scenario.md §2 |
| 2026-11-03 | Precise geolocation, and why background location is treated differently from location used while the app is open | State consumer privacy laws — sensitive data; FTC Act §5 — location data | _Brittany to write in own words._ **Sourced:** "Precise geolocation data" = location "within a radius of 1,750 feet" (§541.001(21) [S01]) and is "sensitive data" (§541.001(29)(D) [S01]). Processing sensitive data requires the consumer's consent (§541.101(b)(4) [S01]), and "consent" excludes acceptance of general terms of use (§541.001(6) [S01]). FTC actions (InMarket, S05–S06; X-Mode, S04) involved location data used for advertising without informed consent. Why it matters here: features F-2 and F-3. | scenario.md §4 |
| 2026-11-03 | FTC Act §5 "unfairness" has a three-part test: substantial injury, not reasonably avoidable by consumers, and not outweighed by benefits | FTC Act §5 — unfair and deceptive practices | _Brittany to write in own words._ **Sourced:** 15 U.S.C. §45(a)(1), (n) [S03]. Why it matters here: the FTC used §5 in the location-data cases (S04–S06). | sources S03–S06 |
| 2026-11-03 | "Targeted advertising" under Texas law is narrow: ads based only on activity within the company's own app are excluded | State consumer privacy laws — targeted advertising and opt-outs | _Brittany to write in own words._ **Sourced:** §541.001(31)(A)(i) [S01]. Why it matters here: whether F-7 (local ads) counts depends on whether the ad network uses data from other apps. | scenario.md F-7 |
| 2026-11-03 | Inherent vs. residual risk: inherent = risk of the design as proposed; residual = risk left after mitigations. A PIA's value is moving risk from inherent to residual by changing the design, not just documenting it. | Privacy program management — PIAs; privacy by design | _Brittany to write in own words._ Why it matters here: four design decisions took 8 High risks to 0 High (risk-register.md, Ratings). | working/risk-register.md |
