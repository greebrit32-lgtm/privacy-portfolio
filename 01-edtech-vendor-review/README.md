# Project 01 — EdTech Vendor Privacy Review: Wayground

**Scenario:** I'm a privacy analyst at a fictional Texas school district (details in [scenario.md](scenario.md), drafted in Session 2). The district needs a review of Wayground, a classroom quiz and assessment platform, before teachers can use it. The review uses **only the vendor's public documents**.

**Benchmarks:** FERPA (including the school-official exception), COPPA, Texas Education Code Ch. 32 Subch. D, and the Student Data Privacy Consortium (SDPC) National Data Privacy Agreement.

## Deliverables
1. `deliverables/1-vendor-questionnaire.xlsx`: vendor privacy questionnaire with a scoring rubric
2. `deliverables/2-risk-assessment.md`: risk-rated assessment and findings table
3. `deliverables/3-privacy-officer-memo.md`: 2-page memo to the district privacy officer

## How the work is organized
- `sources/`: source register (URL, access date, document version) and dated snapshots
- `working/`: requirements matrix and evidence notes, which link each source to its finding
- `session-log.md`: progress log and next step
- `study-notes.md`: CIPP/US concepts that came up along the way
