Wayground Guiding Privacy Principles
Respecting your privacy and the security of your personal information and data is critically important to achieving our mission of motivating every student. These are our guiding privacy principles:
We don’t ask you for personal information unless we truly need it.
We don’t retain your personal data longer than needed to make our services work for you.
We don’t share your personal information except to comply with the law or to provide our services.
We don’t rent, sell, or exchange your personal information.
We make it simple for you to control what is visible to the public, kept private, indexed by third parties, or permanently deleted.
General Privacy Policy Information
This Privacy Policy and Privacy Notice (referred to herein as “Privacy Policy”) applies to users of the Wayground platform (formerly Quizizz) and describes how Quizizz Inc., doing business as Wayground ("Wayground" or "Quizizz"), collects, uses, shares, stores, and protects your personally identifiable information—and how you can access, update, delete, or limit the use of your information.
The Wayground platform includes its website, quizzes, lessons, practice tests, instructional tools, educational content, integrations, and related products and services, such as Voyage Math™, Quizizz for Work™, Quizizz AI™, and user support (collectively referred to as the "Services"), and is hosted and operated by Quizizz Inc. Quizizz Inc. also operates other products and services under separate brands, including the Skillet mobile and web applications and related services. Unless expressly stated otherwise, this Privacy Policy applies only to the Services and does not govern the privacy practices of any other Quizizz Inc. product or service.
Wayground offers you choices regarding the collection, use, and sharing of your personal information, including the option to use certain Services without providing personal data. More details and answers to frequently asked questions are available in our Privacy Center .
By using Wayground products and services, you consent to the terms described in this Privacy Policy. Your continued use of the Wayground platform, website, and applications constitutes your acceptance of these terms and your consent to Wayground handling your personal information as described.
Wayground is committed to accessibility for all users, including those with disabilities. If a screen reader makes this policy more accessible, we recommend the following browser and screen reader combinations: Chrome with ChromeVox, Safari with VoiceOver (Mac), and Firefox with NVDA (Windows).

Section 1. Protecting Students and their Personal Information
Wayground believes the personally identifiable information of children and students deserves special protections. Wayground complies with relevant laws and regulations in all of the areas we operate:

In the U.S, the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (“COPPA”), the general consumer data privacy and student advertising limitation laws of US states
In Canada, the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (“PIPEDA”) and other relevant federal and provincial laws, (see 15.2 below)
In the EU, the General Data Protection Regulation (“GDPR”) and country, state and province regulations for all countries with Wayground users
We also assist schools in meeting their obligations pursuant to the Family Educational Rights Privacy Act (“FERPA”). We recognize that information crossing borders may be subject to the laws of multiple jurisdictions.

These are some examples of safety and privacy measures on Wayground:

Limited feature set and website experience for younger users
No requirement for students to submit name/contact info or create accounts
For users under the age of consent, we do not collect personal information as defined under COPPA
Except as described in this privacy policy, Wayground does not accumulate personal information about any child for distribution, sharing, or selling
Students do not receive behaviorally targeted advertising.
Students can join games without creating an account.
The platform does not facilitate direct messaging between students.
Activities are subject to proactive content filters and reactive reporting tools.
Section 2. Parental Rights and Consents
Wayground empowers parents and legal guardians of children younger than the age of consent (in the U.S., this means under 13), who have registered accounts, to exercise their legal rights pursuant to legislation, such as US federal statute COPPA.

Parents/guardians can consent to collection and use of a child's personally identifiable information (“PII”) by Wayground without consenting to the disclosure of that information to third parties.

Currently, the only PII we collect from a child younger than the age of consent in any of the jurisdictions we operate in is their parent's or guardian’s email address. We may also collect a student’s first name, which alone is generally not PII but may be considered personal information in certain jurisdictions.

Wayground provides consent options to parents regarding the collection and use of PII or other personal information (“PI”) necessary to provide the service without consenting to the use or disclosure of that information to third parties for other purposes (e.g., marketing).

Additional safeguards that parents may use:
Parents/guardians may delete, modify, and review PI their children shared with Wayground by accessing their child's account.
Parents may also create a parent account to manage their child’s access to Wayground.
Parents may also request that Wayground delete that information and refuse to allow Wayground to collect further information from their children by deleting their account at wayground.com/settings or otherwise contacting us at privacy@quizizz.com
Wayground may ask additional questions or take other steps to verify the identity of parents/guardians before responding to a request to review or delete their child's information, or a request to refuse further collection from children.
As necessary, Wayground ensures that there is verifiable parental consent for children younger than the age of consent in any jurisdiction. We never require a child to share more information than is reasonably necessary for participation. Wayground uses, discloses and retains personal information only for the purpose of providing the classroom web app/software/service.

While Wayground will assign persistent identifiers to all Wayground users to recognize repeat visitors and improve their Wayground experience, Wayground does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children younger than the age of consent without a parent or guardian's consent.

If we become aware, despite our intention not to gather this information, that we have collected personally identifiable information from a child younger than the age of consent without permission, then we will seek to obtain verifiable parental consent from the child’s parent or guardian to collect and use that data to provide the child with Wayground services in the future; or, if consent is not provided, we will delete this information
Section 3. Information Collected By Wayground
3.1. Sensitive Information
As described above, we limit data collection to what we need to provide the requested services. Wayground does not collect data considered or defined as sensitive under any relevant country and state laws including COPPA, FERPA, CCPA, and other laws of the US, PIPEDA and related federal and provincial laws in Canada, GDPR, and related laws in the EU, UK, Switzerland, and EEA, and similar laws in Australia, Brazil and other countries in which Wayground provides its service, such as those related to race or ethnicity, health and biometric information, or political affiliation.

In addition to these categories, we do not collect information on free or reduced lunch eligibility.
3.2 Information You Provide to Wayground
Accounts are not a prerequisite to using Wayground, but accounts are required to access some features and to save progress over time. If you create an account, Wayground saves the account information you provide. This information may include: an email address, a parent or guardian email address, first name, last name, profile photo, or organization name.

Student profiles and activity within the Wayground app and service are kept private and cannot be seen or collected by others unless their use of the app/service itself is collaborative and requires this type of sharing.

You may choose not to provide specific information or to delete or modify it at any time. These actions may prevent you from engaging with specific services or features that require this information.

You may be providing additional information to Wayground if you communicate with our support team, participate in user research, marketing campaigns or sweepstakes, or otherwise use the services. We only collect this type of information from adults and non-students users and we do not permit minors, children or students to participate in user research, marketing campaigns or sweepstakes, or otherwise use the services.

If you engage in a financial transaction with Wayground, we do not process or store this information beyond the transaction. Financial transactions are processed via a PCI-compliant payment provider and data is not stored by Wayground.

As you use the service, you may also create academic content including quizzes and certain forms of responses. This information is not typically personally identifying, but it may be deleted at any time either by the relevant account holder or by reaching out to our team at support@quizizz.com.

3.3. Information Collected Automatically
We receive and store some types of information about how users visit our site and the devices they use automatically as you use the service. These may include device information such as device model, operating system, and device identifiers such as IDFA and UDID and network information.
Wayground does not collect any personal information covertly, i.e., without the user’s knowledge, particularly audio/video information via the user’s own device.
Wayground also collects log information as you use the service. These data may include information regarding how and when you use the service as well as your browser type, preferred language, time zone, referring site, log-in and log-out times, and internet protocol (IP) address.
We may use your IP address to generate a general approximation of your location to provide the service. Examples include providing the right version of Wayground based on your state or country and helping teachers select their school when they sign up. Wayground does not collect fine location data such as GPS or bluetooth beacons.
We use Google Analytics to help us understand how users engage with the service. Google Analytics uses cookies to compile reports that demonstrate how Wayground is being used without identifying individual visitors. You can opt out of Google Analytics at any time and continue to use Wayground. To learn more about opting out, click here .
Aggregated demographic and activity data may be used for research purposes.
Section 4. Information We Store; Data Retention
We will retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you services. We conduct periodic reviews of accounts and may delete accounts that have been inactive for extended periods of time, normally not sooner than one year.
We may retain and use your information as necessary to comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.
You may delete your account or update your information at any time by visiting www.wayground.com/settings

5.2. Third Party Cloud Provider: Artificial Intelligence (AI) Platforms
A topic currently in the news and of particular concern and interest to Parents and Users is how Wayground uses Artificial Intelligence (AI) platforms.
Wayground does NOT use AI platforms or sites for any personal data of our website visitors or users, and we do not transmit any personal data in connection with those uses.
The data referred to for AI purposes is educational materials produced by our users and transmitted in the form of “data” that allows the AI models to generate additional content.
Wayground uses third party AI platforms to help build its users’ quizzes and other educational materials more quickly and with broader scope of research than was previously available. Wayground products can now integrate with some of the major providers of public generative AI services, such as OpenAI,  Anthropic or others. Wayground has set up associated APIs and features (“AI Tools”) with some of these providers.
These are the main guidelines under which Wayground employs AI integrations.
Use of AI Tools.
‍
We allow our Users to create with AI tools to expand the scope of Wayground products and services and enhance their User experience.
AI Tools may give our users the ability to more quickly generate questions, lesson plans, quizzes, assessments and other educational materials, assist in creating communications to other educators and parents, and assist in planning, presentations and expanded uses of Wayground products.
All information provided to these AI providers is securely transmitted, stored in the US, and encrypted.

No Data Sharing or Use for Training AI.

Information uploaded by Wayground users is only used to create educational materials and enhance our services.
Results of our AI Tools are only visible to Wayground users for their own searches, unless our users choose to make the educational materials they create public on the Wayground platform.
With all of our AI providers, we have disabled their use of any Wayground user data for training their own large language models (LLMs), and our AI providers do not use our data to train their LLMs.

Opt-in Feature and User Rights.
‍
The use of AI Tools is a Wayground default feature, which means AI Tools may be automatically integrated into any or all of our products and services to enhance the user experience and creation of educational materials.
If any Wayground user wishes to opt-out of the AI tools feature, the User may opt-out by choosing not to use any of our AI tools, which are clearly labeled as such.

Data Collection, Processing and Security.
‍
We maintain industry-standard security protocols to ensure the security and confidentiality of the data collected by AI tools we employ for expanding the scope of our products and services.
Our security practices are governed by applicable data privacy laws and supported by independent security certifications and assessments. Wayground maintains ISO 27001 certification and has completed a SOC 2® Type II examination conducted by an independent service auditor, reflecting our ongoing commitment to maintaining robust security measures.
The data collected and processed by AI tools is educational data for use in allowing AI tools to expand the scope and functionality of our products and servers and to allow our users to generate content more easily and quickly using AI in an assistant function.

Section 6. How Wayground Collects Minimum Necessary Personal Information
Wayground collects only the personal information required to operate its classroom web apps, software and services. It does not access browser history, contact lists, search terms, preferences, device identification, location, etc. unless directly related to providing the service.
Wayground does not condition a child's participation in a game, the offering of a prize, or ability to participate in any other activity on the child disclosing more personal information than is reasonably necessary to participate in such activity.
The information that Wayground collects depends on the nature of the services you request. For example, fewer data are collected from a student joining a game without an account than from a teacher who creates an account in order to create quizzes and host games. Educators using Wayground may create generic accounts or profiles for children using as little personal information as possible.
All interactions with the Wayground website or app produce metadata that may identify you or could be identifiable information when paired with other information about you. In every case, Wayground strives to collect only the minimum necessary information from you as is necessary or appropriate to fulfill the purpose of the visitor's interaction with Wayground or that is required by law. Visitors to the Wayground website or app may always refuse to supply personally identifying information, however, doing so may prevent you from engaging in certain website-related activities.
