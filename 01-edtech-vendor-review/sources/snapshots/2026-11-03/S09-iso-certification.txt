Wayground ISO Certification

Updated 4 Nov 2025

2 min read

Overview
At Wayground, we prioritize the security and privacy of our users' data. To demonstrate our commitment to maintaining the highest standards of information security, we have achieved ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification. This globally recognized standard ensures that our Information Security Management System (ISMS) is robust and effective in managing and protecting information assets.

What is ISO/IEC 27001:2022?
ISO/IEC 27001:2022 is an international standard for managing information security. It provides a framework for establishing, implementing, maintaining, and continually improving an ISMS. The standard helps organizations protect their information assets by addressing people, processes, and technology.

Why This Matters to Users
ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification is crucial for several reasons:
Achieving ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification has several direct benefits for our users:
Enhanced Security : Your sensitive information is comprehensively protected through stringent security measures, reducing the risk of data breaches.
Risk Management : Potential security risks are identified and mitigated, ensuring that your data remains safe from threats.
Compliance : We meet all legal, regulatory, and contractual requirements, giving you peace of mind that your data is handled according to global standards.
Building Trust : Our dedication to safeguarding your data builds trust, reassuring you that your information is in safe hands.
Continuous Improvement : Our commitment to ongoing assessment and improvement of security practices means we are always working to protect your data better.
Our Commitment to Security
Achieving ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification is a testament to our dedication to maintaining the highest standards of information security. Our ISMS covers all aspects of our operations, including:
Access Control: Ensuring only authorized personnel have access to sensitive information.
Data Encryption:  Protecting data through encryption methods.
Incident Management:  Responding swiftly and effectively to security incidents.
Regular Audits: Conducting internal and external audits to ensure compliance with the standard.
Employee Training: We provide continuous training to our employees on best practices for security.
You can find our ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification listed within on this  Certification and Attestation Register . You can view our certification details and the controls we continuously monitor  here .

Contact Us
If you have any questions about our ISO certification or our information security practices, please contact us at privacy@wayground.com
