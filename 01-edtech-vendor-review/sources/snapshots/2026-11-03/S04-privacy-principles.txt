The Wayground Privacy Principles :

The Wayground Privacy Principles

Updated 4 Nov 2025

1 min read

Respecting your privacy and the security of your personal information and data is critically important to achieving our mission of motivating every student. These are our guiding privacy principles:
✔️ We don’t ask you for personal information unless we truly need
✔️ We don’t keep your personal data, personal information or personally identifiable information longer than needed to make Wayground products work for you
✔️ We don’t share your personal information with anyone except to comply with the law or as needed to provide our services
✔️ We don’t rent, sell or exchange your personal information
✔️ We make it simple for you to control what is (a) visible to the public, (b) kept private, (c) seen by any third-party search engines, browsers, platforms, websites, apps, or processors, and (d) permanently deleted
