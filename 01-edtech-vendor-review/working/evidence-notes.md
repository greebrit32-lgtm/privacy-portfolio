## Scoring frame (decided by Brittany, 2026-11-03)
- **Score** = what the public documents provide that Pecan Hollow ISD could rely on. That includes the TX-NDPA v1r6 terms in S12, which a Texas district can adopt by signing Exhibit E (S06, Texas row).

## Key observations so far (facts only)
1. **AI text unchanged since the Quizizz era.** The AI section of the current policy (S01 §5.2) matches the Quizizz policy effective 2024-12-10 (S14 §5.2) in every sentence compared, including naming "OpenAI, Anthropic or others." Statements found in S01 but not in S14 include the ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Type II references (S01 §5.2, §12.1).
2. **AI provider names differ across documents.** S01 §5.2 names OpenAI and Anthropic as examples. The S05 "AI service providers" table lists Portkey, Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud Platform, and Amazon Web Services. S11 says Wayground AI "combines GPT-5." S10 names Pangram as the integrity provider. Pangram, OpenAI, and Anthropic are not in S05.
3. **What reaches AI processing.** S01 §5.2 says Wayground does "NOT use AI platforms … for any personal data" and that "the data referred to for AI purposes is educational materials." S10 says the Wayground AI extension reads "Classroom rosters" ("Showing student names") and "student submissions" ("Reading work for grading and feedback"), with "Student submission content retention: deleted within 72 hours of processing." The public documents do not explain how these statements relate (e.g., whether submissions are de-identified before AI processing).
4. **Who can use AI.** S10 says the extension is for "Teachers only; student accounts are auto-blocked." S01 §15.2 (in the Canada section; §15 says these provisions "will generally apply as additional privacy protections for all users") lists "limiting the use of AI tools as a Wayground default feature for teachers and admins only." S11 describes student-facing supports ("Reading supports load automatically for students who need them. Translations happen seamlessly") without saying whether those use AI.
5. **Legacy agreement.** S12 (TX-NDPA v1r6) was signed by the provider as "Quizizz" on 06/11/2025 and by the district on 6/17/2025 (S12 p. 3). The vendor's DBA letter is dated 2025-06-05 (S25). S08 says "any contracts signed with Quizizz Inc. remain valid." **S12 Exhibit A (products and services covered; pp. 10–11) is blank or illegible in the public scan.** S12 Art. IV §2 limits use to the services "outlined in Exhibit 'A'."
6. **Data schedule versus AI features.** In S12 Exhibit B (pp. 12–14) as executed, "Student work (Student generated content; writing, pictures, etc.)" and "Student Name (First and/or Last)" are **not** checked. S10 describes the AI extension reading student submissions and names.
